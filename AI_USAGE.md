# AI-assisted development disclosure

AI-assisted coding and documentation tools were used during private development and public-release
preparation. The human author selected the research scope, reviewed changes, ran verification, and
accepts responsibility for the released content.

Restricted datasets, raw media, human-subject records, credentials, and item-level real predictions
are not included in this repository. AI assistance was constrained to code, documentation,
synthetic fixtures, and privacy-minimized aggregate information. Generated suggestions are not
treated as scientific evidence and do not replace human authorship or accountability.
