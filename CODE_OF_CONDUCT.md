# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free collaboration environment. Be considerate,
specific, and evidence-oriented; critique ideas and implementations rather than people. Do not
publish another person's private information or restricted research material.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sustained disruption,
and disclosure of private or restricted information. Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject
contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when needed to protect the
community or project.

Report conduct concerns privately using GitHub's repository-owner contact mechanism. The maintainer
will acknowledge reports, minimize access to the report, investigate proportionately, and document
any enforcement decision without exposing confidential details.

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project elsewhere.
